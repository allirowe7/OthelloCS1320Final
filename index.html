Othello Rules

  • Objective: Have the majority of your color discs on the board at the end of the game.
  • Gameplay: Players alternate turns placing one disc of their color on the board.
  • Valid Move: You must place your disc adjacent to an opponent's disc and flip one or more of their discs by surrounding them.
  • Flipping Discs: When you place a disc so that your discs surround one or more opponent's discs in a straight line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal), those opponent's discs are flipped to your color.
  • No Valid Moves: If a player cannot make a valid move, their turn is skipped. If neither player can make a valid move, the game ends.
  • Game End: The game ends when the board is full or neither player can make a valid move. The player with the most discs of their color wins.
